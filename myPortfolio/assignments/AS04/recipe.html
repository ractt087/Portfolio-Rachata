Pizza Dip

Ingredients

FOR THE PULL-APART BREAD

  • 16.3 ounce can Grands biscuit dough, 8 count
  • ¼ cup melted butter
  • 2 teaspoons parmesan cheese, grated
  • 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning

FOR THE PIZZA DIP

  • 8 ounces cream cheese, softened
  • 1 cup ricotta cheese
  • 3 teaspoons Italian seasoning, divided
  • 1 cup marinara sauce, or pizza sauce
  • 1 ½ cups mozzarella cheese, grated
  • 8 slices pepperoni, quartered (optional topping)

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F. Cut each biscuit in half, then roll each half of dough into a ball.
  2. Combine the melted butter, parmesan cheese, and 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning in a bowl. Roll each ball of biscuit dough in the seasoned butter.
  3. Arrange them around the edge of a greased large cast iron skillet.
  4. In a large bowl, combine cream cheese, ricotta cheese, and 2 teaspoons Italian seasoning. Spread in the middle of the skillet.
  5. Top with marinara sauce.
  6. Top with shredded mozzarella cheese and remaining 1 teaspoon Italian seasoning.
  7. Top with pepperonis or another pizza topping of your choice.
  8. Place in the oven and bake for 25-30 minutes until golden brown.

Finished Dish!

Serve hot and enjoy dipping!
